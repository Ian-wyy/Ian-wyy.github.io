Heterogeneous routing, one field, three robots

Yiyu Wang → PyRCH demo

A drone, a wheeled robot and a legged robot leave the same depot and have to visit every target between them. They are not interchangeable: the drone flies straight over the river and the rocks, the wheeled robot is the fastest but has to take the bridge and cannot enter the rocks, and the legged robot is slow but is the only ground robot that can walk into them. PyRCH minimises the makespan — the time the last robot gets home — so the plan balances travel time, not distance. Type a seed — or roll a random one — and watch the three of them run on one shared clock.
